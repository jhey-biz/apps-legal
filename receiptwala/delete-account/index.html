Delete your ReceiptWala account

This page explains how to delete your ReceiptWala account and what happens to your data, as required by Google Play's account deletion policy.

App: ReceiptWala (com.receiptwala.app), published by Neobplan.

Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open ReceiptWala and sign in if you aren't already.
  2. Tap the Account icon at the top of the receipt list.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Can't access the app?

Email support@neo-b.net from the email address (or including the phone number) linked to your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We verify ownership and complete deletion within 7 days, then confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (phone / Google / Truecaller / email identity)
  • Your cloud profile (locale, consent timestamps)
  • Your subscription record (Play subscriptions themselves are managed and cancelled in the Google Play app)

What is NOT touched — because we never had it

  • Receipts on your phone stay on your phone. Uninstall the app or delete them inside the app if you want them gone.
  • Backups in your own Google Drive belong to your Google account, not us. Delete the ReceiptWala-Backups folder in your Drive if you no longer want them.

Details: see our Privacy Policy.
